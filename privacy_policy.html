Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

At Spike, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information when you use our platform.

🔒 Our Promise: We will never sell your personal data. Your trust is our priority.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Data: Name, email address, SP ID, profile information, and account settings.
  • Usage Data: How you interact with Spike, including messages, rooms, and features you use.
  • Device Data: Device type, browser version, IP address, and operating system for analytics and security.
  • Content: Messages, posts, and files you choose to share on the platform.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To Provide Services: To operate, maintain, and improve Spike.
  • To Communicate: To send you updates, security alerts, and support messages.
  • To Personalize: To tailor your experience and show relevant content.
  • For Security: To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access.
  • To Comply with Law: To meet legal obligations and respond to lawful requests.

3. Data Storage & Security

  • Secure Infrastructure: Your data is stored on secured servers with industry-standard encryption.
  • Access Control: Only authorized personnel have access to user data, and only when necessary.
  • Data Retention: We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion at any time.

4. Sharing Your Information

  • We Do Not Sell Your Data: We never sell or rent your personal information to third parties.
  • Service Providers: We may share data with trusted partners who help us operate Spike (e.g., hosting, analytics).
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights.
  • Aggregated Data: We may share anonymized, aggregated data for research or analytics purposes.

5. Your Choices & Rights

  • Access & Update: You can view and update your profile information at any time.
  • Delete Account: You may request account deletion. Your SP ID will be permanently retired.
  • Opt-Out: You can opt out of promotional emails at any time.
  • Data Portability: You may request a copy of your data in a portable format.

6. Cookies & Tracking

  • Essential Cookies: Used for authentication and platform functionality.
  • Analytics: We use cookies to understand usage patterns and improve our service.
  • Preferences: Cookies store your settings and preferences for a better experience.
  • Control: You can manage cookies in your browser settings.

7. Children's Privacy

Spike is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided data to us, please contact us immediately.

8. International Data Transfers

Your data may be stored and processed in countries outside your jurisdiction. We take steps to ensure your data is protected in accordance with this Privacy Policy and applicable laws.

9. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via email or platform notification. Your continued use of Spike constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding your privacy, please contact us at:
privacy@spike.com
We will respond to all inquiries within 30 days.

Your privacy is not just a policy—it's our promise.
