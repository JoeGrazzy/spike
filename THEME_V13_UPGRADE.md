# SPIKE Theme v13 Upgrade
Canonical tokens: --bg, --card-bg, --card-border, --accent, --accent-glow, --secondary-accent, --text, --text-muted.

Theme 1 Electric Cyan; 2 Neon Purple; 3 Blazing Orange; 4 Mint Emerald; 5 Royal Blue; 6 Warm Rose Light; 7 Lavender; 8 Glacier Cyan Light; 9 Metallic Gold; 10 Crimson OLED.

Compatibility aliases preserve legacy --spike-* and older surface/accent variables. `theme-v13.css` is loaded last on every HTML page so old page-level theme definitions no longer win the cascade.
