🔒 Safety, privacy & account security

Protect your information and use SPIKE responsibly.

Report content or accounts

Use the report control on the relevant post, profile, message or activity to raise a concern.

Block unwanted contact

Use blocking controls when you no longer want to interact with an account. Review the confirmation before proceeding.

Share carefully

Only share information you are comfortable making available to the selected audience. Avoid posting private contact, financial or identity details.

Protect sign-in details

Never share your password, one-time verification codes, recovery links or private keys with another person or support contact.

Check suspicious links

Be cautious with unfamiliar links, unexpected prizes and requests for sensitive information. Use official SPIKE pages to sign in.

Privacy controls

Review the privacy and audience options available in your account settings. Server-side privacy rules may restrict what other members can see.

Review settings →

🛠️ Troubleshooting

Try these safe first steps before repeating an action.

A page or profile keeps loading

Check your connection, wait briefly and refresh once. If it persists, sign out and back in. Avoid repeatedly submitting forms or actions while the network is unstable.

Notifications or live data look stale

Check that you are online and signed in to the correct account, then refresh once. If the issue continues, contact support with the page name and approximate time.

I cannot upload a photo or video

Check the file type and size, connection quality and browser permissions. Try a supported file and an up-to-date browser.

Messages are not updating

Check your connection, reopen the conversation and refresh once. If necessary, sign out and back in. Do not send the same message repeatedly while waiting.

A feature is missing

Some features depend on account status, device capabilities, browser permissions, privacy settings or available data. Update your browser and follow any in-app prompts.

I see unexpected language or translated text

Check your browser's translation setting and disable automatic translation for SPIKE if it is changing the page. Reload the page after changing the setting.

❓ Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to common account and app questions.

How do I reset my password?

Use the password recovery option on the sign-in screen and follow the official reset instructions.

Why was I signed out?

Your session may have expired or become invalid. Sign in again and check your connection if it happens repeatedly.

Why can I not see another member's profile?

Their privacy settings, your account state or a discovery restriction may limit access. A profile being unavailable does not necessarily mean it has been deleted.

Can I guarantee that a post will rank highly?

No. Reach and ranking are not guaranteed. Focus on original, useful content and genuine participation.

What should I include when contacting support?

Describe what you were trying to do, the page or feature, what happened, the approximate time and your browser/device type. Never include passwords, verification codes or private keys.
