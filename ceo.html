About SPIKE

SPIKE is a social platform created to help people connect, communicate, share experiences, and interact through modern digital features.

Social Feed Chat Voice Calls Video Calls

The Story Behind SPIKE

Mr. Elechi Joshua was inspired by his passion for social life and human connection. Instead of only imagining an idea, he decided to take action and build a platform from scratch.

What some people saw as spending time on a phone was actually time dedicated to learning, designing, testing, and creating a real product.

The Commitment Behind The Vision

Building SPIKE required dedication, patience, and personal sacrifice. Resources were committed toward development, including internet access, research, testing, and the tools needed throughout the journey.

Vision For The Future

The vision is for SPIKE to grow into a platform that connects people around the world and creates meaningful digital experiences.

Founder Message

"I created SPIKE because I believe in the power of connection. What started as an idea became a journey of building, learning, and believing in something bigger. My mission is to continue improving SPIKE and create a platform that people can enjoy, trust, and be proud to be part of."

— Mr. Elechi Joshua
