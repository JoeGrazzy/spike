POL-CORE

1. Policy principles

SPIKE applies its rules consistently, with context and proportionality where appropriate.

Source of truth POL-CORE-001
Moderation, recommendation safety, notifications, appeals and administrative tools should reference this policy framework and its version rather than maintaining conflicting copies of the rules.
Context and proportionality POL-CORE-002
A detection signal is not automatically proof of a violation. Systems should distinguish clear violations from context-dependent cases and route uncertain cases to review when appropriate.
Safety and fairness POL-CORE-003
SPIKE should prioritize user safety while minimizing unnecessary removals, discriminatory enforcement and avoidable false positives.
POL-SAFETY

2. Safety

Content that creates serious safety risks may be removed immediately or escalated for review.

Violence and credible threats POL-SAFETY-001
Threats of serious physical harm, targeted incitement of violence and credible plans to harm people are prohibited. Immediate-risk cases may receive priority escalation.
Child safety POL-SAFETY-002
Child sexual exploitation and sexualization of minors are prohibited. Suspected material should be handled through the highest-safety moderation path and appropriate escalation procedures.
Self-harm encouragement POL-SAFETY-003
Encouraging, glorifying or facilitating self-harm or suicide is prohibited. Supportive discussion and requests for help are not treated as equivalent to encouragement.
Terrorism and extremist violence POL-SAFETY-004
Recruitment, operational support, credible praise intended to advance violent extremist activity and instructions that facilitate violent extremist acts are prohibited.
POL-CONTENT

3. Prohibited content

SPIKE may restrict or remove content that falls within defined prohibited categories.

Hateful conduct POL-CONTENT-001
Attacks, dehumanization or calls for harm targeting people because of protected characteristics are prohibited. Discussion, criticism, reporting or educational context can require contextual review.
Targeted harassment POL-CONTENT-002
Sustained targeted abuse, threats, humiliation campaigns and coordinated harassment are prohibited. Ordinary disagreement or criticism is not automatically harassment.
Sexual exploitation POL-CONTENT-003
Sexual exploitation, non-consensual sexual imagery and sexual content involving minors are prohibited. Some adult sexual content may also be restricted under product-specific rules.
Fraud and harmful deception POL-CONTENT-004
Scams, impersonation for fraud, credential theft, malicious payment schemes and deceptive activity designed to cause material harm are prohibited.
Prohibited words and phrases POL-CONTENT-005
SPIKE may maintain a server-side prohibited-term policy list. Terms are detection signals mapped to policy IDs; a match may trigger automatic blocking when the rule is unambiguous, or contextual review when meaning depends on surrounding content.
POL-CONDUCT

4. Community conduct

Users are expected to participate without abusing people or the platform.

Impersonation and deceptive identity POL-CONDUCT-001
Impersonating another person, organization or SPIKE administrator in a way that deceives or harms others is prohibited.
Spam and manipulation POL-CONDUCT-002
Coordinated spam, artificial engagement, automated abuse, recommendation manipulation and attempts to evade enforcement are prohibited.
Doxxing and privacy abuse POL-CONDUCT-003
Publishing or soliciting highly sensitive personal information for harassment, intimidation or harm is prohibited.
POL-PRIVACY

5. Privacy

SPIKE should protect private information and respect access boundaries.

Private information POL-PRIVACY-001
Users must not expose another person's sensitive personal information without an appropriate lawful and legitimate basis. Admin access to private moderation data must be permission-controlled and audited.
Private communications POL-PRIVACY-002
Private messages and private community data should not be exposed through public recommendation, search or administrative interfaces except where an authorized safety or legal workflow requires access.
POL-PLATFORM

6. Platform abuse

Attempts to compromise SPIKE or evade its controls are prohibited.

Security abuse POL-PLATFORM-001
Unauthorized access, credential theft, malware distribution, exploitation of security controls and deliberate disruption of SPIKE are prohibited.
Enforcement evasion POL-PLATFORM-002
Creating or using accounts, content or technical mechanisms primarily to evade a valid SPIKE enforcement action may result in additional restrictions.
POL-ENFORCEMENT

7. Enforcement

Enforcement is tied to the policy violation and the severity, confidence and history of the case.

DetectSignals from rules, reports or systems.
ClassifyMap to a policy ID.
ActRemove, restrict or review.
NotifyExplain the decision.
AppealAllow eligible review.
Possible actions POL-ENFORCEMENT-001
  • Warning or educational notice
  • Content restriction or removal
  • Temporary feature restriction
  • Temporary account suspension
  • Permanent account enforcement for severe or repeated violations
Automatic takedown POL-ENFORCEMENT-002
Automatic removal should be reserved for rules with sufficiently high confidence. The moderation event should preserve the matched policy ID, policy version, decision source, timestamp and relevant evidence.
POL-APPEALS

8. Appeals

Eligible users can challenge an enforcement decision through the SPIKE appeal workflow.

Appeal eligibility POL-APPEALS-001
The removal or restriction notice should identify whether an appeal is available. An appeal should reference the original moderation case rather than creating an unrelated case.
Review states POL-APPEALS-002
The canonical workflow is pending → under review → approved or rejected. The system should prevent duplicate active appeals for the same moderation case.
Appeal decisions POL-APPEALS-003
An approved appeal can restore eligible content and overturn the original decision. A rejected appeal leaves the enforcement in place. Both outcomes must be recorded in an immutable audit trail.
Appeals are a separate decision layer. An appeal should never silently edit or erase the original moderation record.
POL-RECOMMENDATIONS

9. Recommendation safety

Recommendation systems must apply policy safety before ranking content for distribution.

Safety before ranking POL-RECOMMENDATIONS-001
Removed or restricted content must not enter recommendation candidates. Policy eligibility is checked before personalization and ranking signals are applied.
Personal mute vs platform prohibition POL-RECOMMENDATIONS-002
A user's personal muted-word preference changes that user's feed. A platform prohibited-content rule changes content eligibility for everyone. These systems must remain separate.
Recommended pipeline: publish/edit → server moderation → remove or review when required → privacy/access checks → personal mute filtering → candidate generation → safety eligibility → ranking → feed.
POL-COPYRIGHT

10. Copyright and intellectual property

Users are responsible for having appropriate rights to content they upload or share.

Rights to uploaded material POL-COPYRIGHT-001
Users should not upload material they do not have permission or a lawful basis to use. SPIKE should provide an appropriate copyright complaint and takedown process.
Repeat infringement POL-COPYRIGHT-002
Repeated valid copyright violations may result in escalating account enforcement under applicable law and SPIKE's terms.
POL-TRANSPARENCY

11. Transparency and accountability

Moderation decisions should be explainable and auditable.

Decision records POL-TRANSPARENCY-001
Every enforcement action should retain its policy ID, policy version, action, decision source, timestamp and sufficient evidence for authorized review.
User notices POL-TRANSPARENCY-002
When content is removed or restricted, the user notification should provide a clear reason and identify the applicable policy category when safe and appropriate to disclose.
Administrative accountability POL-TRANSPARENCY-003
Administrative moderation decisions should be permission-controlled and logged. Privileged tools should not bypass the policy record.
POL-GOVERNANCE

12. Policy changes

Policy changes must be versioned so historical decisions remain interpretable.

Versioning POL-GOVERNANCE-001
Each published policy revision receives a version identifier and effective date. Moderation decisions reference the version that governed the decision.
Change control POL-GOVERNANCE-002
Changes to enforcement-critical rules should be reviewed, logged and propagated to dependent moderation and recommendation systems before being treated as active.
